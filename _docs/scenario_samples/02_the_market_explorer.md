# Scenario 2 — "The Market Explorer"

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Character** | Minh, age 9, Grade 4, learning English |
| **Profile** | Level 12 Vocab Hunter · 87 words captured · 🔥 5-day streak |
| **Setting** | Saturday morning, Bến Thành Market, Hồ Chí Minh City, with grandma |
| **Flow mapping** | §2.1 Collection Loop · §2.2 Rarity & Points · §2.4 Missions |
| **Sections covered** | Returning user camera launch, daily mission announcement, duplicate handling, rare word capture, uncommon word capture, mission completion & bonus, streak continuation |

---

## Context

Minh has been using SnapLearn for three weeks. He is past the thrill of onboarding and deep into the habit — the app is something he opens the way he opens a door, without thinking. Today his grandmother is taking him through Bến Thành Market to buy ingredients for lunch. To Minh, the market is not errands. It is a level.

---

## The Session

### Opening — Camera Ready

Minh's thumb hits the SnapLearn icon as grandma parks the motorbike on Phan Bội Châu street. There is no splash screen, no welcome back dialog, no loading carousel. The viewfinder fills the screen immediately — the camera is live, the capture button pulsing softly at the bottom.

> 🗺️ **Flow mapping — §2.0 Onboarding (skipped):** Onboarding is a first-launch-only experience. For returning users, the app opens directly to the camera viewfinder (§4.2). Minh has not seen the onboarding flow since Week 1.

The top bar shows his avatar (a cartoon pangolin wearing a backpack), his level badge (12 — Vocab Hunter), his point total (1,742 pts), and his streak flame: 🔥 5. The flame icon pulses once when the screen loads. Five days in a row. Minh notices this the way a runner notices their Garmin — a quiet satisfaction, then eyes forward.

The mascot's voice plays over the market noise as the screen finishes loading:

*"Nhiệm vụ hôm nay: tìm 3 thứ bạn có thể ăn!"*
("Today's mission: find 3 things you can eat!")

A mission bar slides in below the viewfinder: **🎯 Food Hunt — 0/3**. Three empty circles. The bar is compact, out of the way, but visible enough that Minh knows exactly what he is chasing.

> 🔊 **Audio-first moment:** The daily mission is announced entirely through TTS. Minh absorbs it while walking, eyes on the market, not on text. The mission bar is a visual confirmation of what his ears already told him. A child who cannot read would receive identical information.

> 💡 **Design principle — Missions as exploration guides (§2.4):** The "Find 3 things you can eat" mission is dynamically generated based on Minh's profile. He has 87 words but only 9 in the Food category — the system is nudging him toward breadth. The mission does not say *which* food to find. It says *go look*. The curriculum is the market itself.

Grandma takes his hand and they step through the main entrance. The noise hits: vendors calling prices, motorbikes idling, a radio playing Vietnamese pop from a fabric stall. The air smells like lemongrass and dried shrimp. Minh holds the phone at his side, viewfinder pointed loosely at the ground, and scans the stalls the way a birdwatcher scans trees — looking for something worth capturing.

---

### Capture 1 — The Familiar Fish

They stop at the seafood aisle. Grandma is examining a pile of mackerel, pressing a thumb into the flesh to test freshness. Minh raises the phone and frames the fish on ice — silvery skin, open mouths, a hand-lettered price sign behind them reading "CÁ THU 85K/KG."

He taps the capture button.

The screen dims for half a second. The loading animation plays: concentric circles rippling outward like sonar, the mascot's voice building anticipation — *"Xem bạn tìm được gì nào..."* ("Let's see what you found..."). Then the card slides up from the bottom of the screen.

A white shimmer. Common ⚪.

The card shows Minh's own photograph — the mackerel on ice, slightly off-center, a little blurry at the edges. Below it:

**cá** — the Vietnamese label, large and clear.

The mascot speaks the scaffolding sequence:

*"Đây là cá. Tiếng Anh là: fish."*
("This is fish. In English: fish.")

The English word appears on the card: **fish** · /fɪʃ/ · Common ⚪ · 10 pts.

But then a second badge appears, pulsing yellow: **ĐÃ CÓ** — "Already owned." Minh captured `fish` two weeks ago when his mother was cooking at home. This is a duplicate.

> 🗺️ **Flow mapping — §2.3 Collection Mechanics (duplicates):** Re-capturing a word the child already owns triggers a "power-up" flow instead of a fresh collection. Duplicates are useful — they drive pronunciation practice and mastery progression — but yield only ¼ of a new capture's XP to keep exploration more rewarding than repetition.

The mascot's tone shifts from excitement to encouragement — warm, steady, coach-like:

*"Bạn đã có 'fish' rồi! Hãy luyện phát âm để nâng cấp thẻ nhé!"*
("You already have 'fish'! Let's practice pronunciation to power up the card!")

The "Say it!" prompt appears. A large microphone icon pulses at the center of the screen. Below it, the phonetic guide: **/fɪʃ/**. Minh presses and holds the push-to-talk button with his thumb — a large, tactile circle at the bottom of the card — and says "fish" into the phone, leaning close to cut through the market noise.

He releases the button. One second of processing. The ASR transcription comes back: "fish." Exact match.

⭐⭐⭐ — three gold stars cascade across the card. The mastery meter on his existing `fish` card ticks from 3/5 to **4/5**. One more perfect pronunciation across a different session and it will evolve.

**+2 XP** floats upward and fades. A quarter of the normal 8 XP for a Common capture. Minh does not seem to mind — the mastery progress is what he watches. The mission bar updates: **🎯 Food Hunt — 1/3**. One circle fills in green. Fish counts as food, duplicate or not.

> 💡 **Design principle — Duplicates are useful, not worthless (§2.3):** Minh earns reduced XP (+2 instead of +8) but gains mastery progression and mission credit. This design prevents frustration ("I already have that one!") while preserving the incentive to seek new words. The mastery system gives duplicates a purpose: every re-encounter is a pronunciation rep, and five reps across different sessions triggers card evolution — the emotional climax of the collection loop.

> 🔊 **Audio-first moment:** The PTT (push-to-talk) button is critical in this environment. Bến Thành Market at 9am on a Saturday is a wall of sound — vendors shouting, motorbikes, music, a hundred conversations overlapping. Voice Activity Detection would trigger on every stall vendor's call. The PTT button means audio is captured only when Minh's thumb is on the glass. He controls when the microphone listens. This is not a limitation — it is a feature.

The card slides away. The camera viewfinder returns. Minh is already looking for the next thing.

---

### Capture 2 — The Rare Lobster

Grandma moves deeper into the seafood section, toward a stall with large aquarium tanks. Minh follows, half-watching the viewfinder. Then he sees them: lobsters. Three of them, dark blue-green, claws banded with rubber bands, moving slowly in the tank under fluorescent light. The price sign reads "TÔM HÙM 650K/KG." Minh does not care about the price. He cares about the rarity.

He raises the phone carefully, framing one lobster against the blue water of the tank. Tap.

The sonar animation plays again. But this time, something different happens. The screen flashes **blue**. A deep, resonant chime sounds — not the gentle white shimmer of a Common word. The card rises from the bottom, and the entire border pulses with a bright cerulean glow. Blue light. Rare 🔵.

The mascot's voice changes register. Higher energy, faster cadence, unmistakably excited:

*"Oa! Bạn tìm được từ hiếm! Tuyệt vời!"*
("Wow! You found a rare word! Amazing!")

The card displays Minh's photograph — the lobster in the tank, claws reaching, a strange alien beauty in the fluorescent light. Below it:

**tôm hùm** — the Vietnamese label.

The mascot delivers the scaffolding:

*"Đây là tôm hùm. Tiếng Anh là: lobster."*
("This is lobster. In English: lobster.")

**lobster** · /ˈlɒb.stər/ · Rare 🔵 · **50 pts**

Minh's eyes go wide. Fifty points. Five times a Common word. The blue glow continues to pulse around the card edges. A small animated mascot in the corner does a backflip.

> 🗺️ **Flow mapping — §2.2 Rarity & Points:** Rarity is determined dynamically. Lobster is Rare because: (1) it appears infrequently in global captures — most children photograph household objects, not live seafood; (2) "lobster" has moderate phonetic complexity for Vietnamese learners (the consonant cluster /lst/ and the unstressed second syllable /stər/); (3) Minh has few Food-category captures, so the category diversity bonus applies.

The "Say it!" prompt appears. Phonetic guide: **/ˈlɒb.stər/**. This one is harder than "fish." Two syllables, the /b/ stopping into /st/, the schwa at the end.

Minh presses the PTT button. He leans into the phone. "Lob-ster," he says, splitting it into careful halves, the way you might step across a stream on two rocks.

ASR returns: "lobster." Exact match.

⭐⭐⭐ — stars explode across the card. Mastery: **1/5**. This is a new word, so it starts at level 1.

**+50 XP** rises from the card in bold blue numerals, larger than the +2 from the fish. The mission bar ticks: **🎯 Food Hunt — 2/3**. Two circles filled. One to go.

Minh grins. He tugs grandma's sleeve. "Bà ơi, con bắt được từ hiếm!" ("Grandma, I caught a rare word!") Grandma nods, smiles, and goes back to negotiating the price of shrimp.

> 💡 **Design principle — Rarity creates emotional peaks (§2.2):** The blue glow, the chime, the mascot's changed voice, the 50-point reward — all of these are calibrated to make Rare captures feel *genuinely special*. The contrast with the quiet white shimmer of the fish two minutes ago is deliberate. Rarity is not just a points multiplier; it is an emotional event. Minh will remember the lobster. He will tell his friends about the lobster. The lobster is a story.

### Capture 3 — The Durian That Completes the Mission

They leave the seafood aisle and cut through the fruit section toward the spice stalls. The smell changes — from brine and ice to sweetness and peel. Bananas hang in bunches from hooks. Rambutans are piled in red mounds. And there, on a table by itself, guarded by a vendor with a cleaver and a knowing smile, sits a whole durian. Pale green, covered in thorns, the size of a football. The most polarizing fruit in Southeast Asia.

Minh knows what it is. He does not know if SnapLearn will.

He points the camera. The durian fills the frame, thorns and all. Tap.

Sonar animation. The screen flashes **green**. A warm, bell-like tone — brighter than Common, gentler than Rare. The card rises with a soft emerald glow around its edges. Uncommon 🟢.

*"Đây là sầu riêng. Tiếng Anh là: durian."*
("This is durian. In English: durian.")

**durian** · /ˈdʊr.i.ən/ · Uncommon 🟢 · **25 pts** · **NEW!**

> 🔊 **Audio-first moment:** The TTS pronunciation of "durian" is particularly important here. Vietnamese children know the fruit intimately — its smell, its taste, the arguments it causes in families — but the English word is foreign. The TTS speaks it clearly: *DOOR-ee-un*. The phonetic guide on the card reads /ˈdʊr.i.ən/. Minh hears the word mapped onto an object he has known his entire life. This is contextual acquisition at its most direct.

The "Say it!" prompt appears. Minh presses the PTT button. "Doo-ree-un," he says.

ASR returns: "durian." Match.

⭐⭐⭐.

Then the screen erupts.

The mission bar hits **🎯 Food Hunt — 3/3**. All three circles fill. The bar expands, turns gold. Confetti bursts from both sides of the screen — tiny animated stars and food icons raining down. The mascot leaps into the center of the screen, arms raised:

*"Hoàn thành nhiệm vụ! Bạn giỏi lắm!"*
("Mission complete! You're so good!")

The reward breakdown slides in:

| Reward | Amount |
|--------|--------|
| Durian capture (Uncommon 🟢, new) | +25 XP |
| Mission bonus (Food Hunt 3/3) | +50 XP |
| Streak bonus (Day 6) | +10 XP |
| **Session total so far** | **+87 XP** |

The streak counter updates: 🔥 **6**. The flame icon grows briefly larger, flickers, settles.

> 🗺️ **Flow mapping — §2.4 Missions + §2.6 Levels & Progression:** Mission completion is a layered reward event. The base XP for the capture (25 pts for Uncommon) stacks with the mission completion bonus (50 XP, as specified in the mission reward table) and the streak continuation bonus (+10 XP). This triple-stack makes mission-completing captures feel disproportionately rewarding — the child learns that finishing a mission is worth more than the sum of its parts. The streak bonus is small but visible, reinforcing the "don't break the chain" psychology.

The confetti fades. The camera viewfinder returns. A subtle banner at the top reads: *"Nhiệm vụ hoàn thành! Tiếp tục khám phá!"* ("Mission complete! Keep exploring!"). The mascot speaks it aloud for good measure.

Minh is still in the market. Grandma still has three more stalls to visit. The daily mission is done, but the camera is ready and the world is full of words. He could capture the rambutan, the cleaver, the woven basket, the plastic stool the vendor sits on. Each one is XP. Each one might be Rare.

> 💡 **Design principle — Post-mission momentum (§2.4):** The app does not gate the session after mission completion. There is no "come back tomorrow" wall. The camera returns immediately, ready for more. Missions are guardrails, not fences. They guide without constraining. Minh's session continues for as long as Minh and grandma are at the market — and every additional capture earns points, builds mastery, and might turn up something Rare or better.

## Rarity Reference

The rarity system governs point values, visual effects, and emotional weight across all captures.

| Rarity | Points | Card border | Sound | Mascot reaction | Examples |
|--------|--------|-------------|-------|-----------------|----------|
| Common ⚪ | 10 pts | White shimmer | Soft chime | Neutral, encouraging | chair, table, cup, fish |
| Uncommon 🟢 | 25 pts | Green glow | Warm bell | Pleased, upbeat | bicycle, umbrella, durian |
| Rare 🔵 | 50 pts | Blue pulse | Deep resonant chime | Excited, fast cadence | telescope, lobster, cactus |
| Epic 🟣 | 100 pts | Purple aurora | Orchestral swell | Astonished, celebratory | stethoscope, abacus |
| Legendary 🟡 | 250 pts | Gold explosion | Fanfare | Ecstatic, voice cracks with joy | fossils, sundial, sextant |
