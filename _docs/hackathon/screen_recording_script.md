# Screen Recording Script (Round 3: Main Stage)

**Purpose:** A pre-recorded screen capture to play on the projector during the main stage pitch. This is NOT the Devpost demo video — this is a tighter, visuals-only recording that accompanies your live narration on stage.

**Critical rule:** NEVER do a live demo on stage. WiFi fails. Projectors lag. Screens are too small. Use this screen recording instead.

---

## Specs

- **Length:** 60-75 seconds (fits the 1:00-2:00 slot in the stage pitch)
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum
- **Audio:** MUTED or app sounds only (no voiceover baked in — you narrate live on stage)
- **Format:** MP4, stored on USB drive AND Google Slides embed AND phone as backup
- **Orientation:** Portrait phone screen with blurred/branded side panels for landscape projection

## Recording Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Account
- Create a demo account with a fun name and avatar
- Pre-capture 5-8 words so the vault looks populated
- Ensure the leaderboard has other entries (seed data)
- Set the XP close to a level-up threshold so the toy excavator capture triggers a level-up

### Step 2: Arrange Objects
- Water bottle: on a clean surface, well-lit
- Earbuds: contrasting background
- Toy excavator: hold it up or place against a plain wall
- Good lighting — natural daylight or bright room, no harsh shadows

### Step 3: Record
- Use phone's built-in screen recorder
- OR mirror to laptop and record with OBS
- Do the full sequence in one continuous take
- Keep movements smooth and deliberate — no frantic swiping
- Pause briefly on each card reveal (let the animation breathe)

### Step 4: Trim
- Cut any dead space between captures
- Speed up navigation transitions (1.5x) if needed
- Keep card reveals at normal speed — that's the money shot
- Add subtle crossfade transitions between captures

### Step 5: Format for Stage
- If phone portrait: add side panels with gradient or brand color (#4DA8FF)
- Add a small "SnapLearn" watermark in the corner
- Export at 1080p MP4
- Test on a projector or external monitor — make sure text is readable from the back of the room

## How It Integrates with Stage Pitch

Your stage pitch is ~3 minutes. The screen recording plays during the middle section while you narrate live:

```
SLIDE 1-3:  Personal story + Problem + Solution (you talk, slides behind you)
            ↓
SCREEN RECORDING PLAYS (60-75 seconds)
            You narrate live over the video:
            "Watch — I point at a water bottle... the AI identifies it...
             the child hears the word... says it back... collected.
             Now something rarer... earbuds... and when they 
             mispronounce... no punishment, just encouragement.
             And now... a toy excavator... Epic rarity...
             that's what makes kids want to explore the world."
            ↓
SLIDE 6-8:  Innovation + Vision + Team (you talk, slides behind you)
```
