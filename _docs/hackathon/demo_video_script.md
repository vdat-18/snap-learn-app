# Demo Video: Instructions + Script (2-3 Minutes)

**Purpose:** This video is uploaded to YouTube/Vimeo and linked in the Devpost submission. Judges who didn't see your live demo will watch this to decide if you advance. It must stand alone — no prior context.

---

## Technical Setup

### Recording Method

**Option A (Recommended): Screen recording + voiceover**
- Record the phone screen using a screen recorder app or mirroring to laptop
- Record voiceover separately with a good microphone (laptop mic or earbuds mic in a quiet room)
- Combine in any video editor (CapCut, iMovie, DaVinci Resolve)

**Option B: Over-the-shoulder camera**
- Someone films you using the app on your phone
- You narrate live while demoing
- Riskier — harder to control quality, audio, and framing

**Option C: Hybrid (Best quality)**
- Screen record the phone demo
- Film a separate 15-second "human moment" clip (you holding the phone, pointing at objects)
- Edit together: human clip for the opening, screen recording for the demo

### Recording Specs
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum (1920×1080)
- **Orientation:** Landscape if screen-mirrored to laptop. Portrait is OK for phone screen recording — add blurred background bars
- **Audio:** Clear voiceover, no background noise. Record in a quiet room, NOT on the hackathon floor
- **Length:** 2:30 – 3:00 (strict — Devpost allows max 3 minutes, but judges appreciate concise)
- **Format:** MP4, upload to YouTube as Unlisted

### Before Recording
- [ ] App is loaded with a fresh account (or cleared data for clean demo)
- [ ] Pre-capture 3-5 words so the Words Vault and Leaderboard have content to show
- [ ] Ensure all demo objects are pre-tested with the AI
- [ ] Notifications silenced on phone
- [ ] Battery full
- [ ] Script printed or on a second screen for reference
- [ ] Record in a quiet room (NOT the hackathon hall)

## Video Script

### SCENE 1: The Hook (0:00 – 0:20)

**Visual:** Black screen with text fading in, or human moment clip (you holding a phone, looking at it thoughtfully).

**Voiceover:**
> "Smartphones have become a part of every child's life. Most kids use them to passively scroll TikTok and YouTube for hours. But what if that same phone could actively teach them?"

**Text on screen (optional):**
> *"What if screen time could be learning time?"*

**Voiceover:**

### SCENE 3: Introducing SnapLearn (0:35 – 0:45)

**Visual:** App logo / mascot animation, then transition to the app's camera screen.

**Voiceover:**
> "This is SnapLearn — Pokémon Go for vocabulary. Kids point their camera at the real world, capture English words, and build a collection. Let me show you how it works."

---

### SCENE 4: Live Demo — Capture 1: Common Object (0:45 – 1:15)

**Visual:** Phone screen recording — camera viewfinder pointed at a water bottle.

**Voiceover:**
> "I point the camera at a water bottle and snap."

*[Tap capture. Wait for card reveal animation.]*

> "The AI identifies the object in under three seconds. 'Chai nước' in Vietnamese, 'Water Bottle' in English. This one's Common — white shimmer — worth ten points."

*[TTS plays automatically.]*

> "The app speaks both words aloud. This is critical — our users are six-year-olds who may not read yet. Everything is audio-first."

*[Tap pronunciation button, say "water bottle".]*

> "Now the child says it back. Our speech recognition checks: did they say it correctly? Three stars. The word is captured and added to their collection."

---

### SCENE 5: Live Demo — Capture 2: Rarity Jump + Pronunciation Retry (1:15 – 1:50)

**Visual:** Camera pointed at earbuds (or another Uncommon/Rare object).

**Voiceover:**
> "Let's try something less common."

*[Snap earbuds.]*

> "Earbuds — Uncommon, green glow, twenty-five points. Rarer objects are worth more. This motivates kids to explore — go to the market, the park, visit a museum."

*[Deliberately mispronounce.]*

> "But what happens when the pronunciation isn't right?"

*[Show retry prompt with encouraging Vietnamese feedback.]*

> "No punishment. The mascot encourages them in Vietnamese — 'Almost! Try again!' This is designed for building confidence."

*[Pronounce correctly on second try.]*

> "Second try, three stars. Collected."

---

### SCENE 6: Live Demo — Capture 3: Rare/Epic Object (1:50 – 2:10)

**Visual:** Camera pointed at toy excavator or another visually striking object.

**Voiceover:**

*[Snap the toy excavator.]*

## Quick Tips

1. **Record the demo first, then do the voiceover.** This gives you control over pacing and lets you re-record the voice without re-doing the demo.
2. **Don't rush.** 3 minutes is enough. Speak at a comfortable pace.
3. **Show, don't tell.** Every claim should have a visual moment backing it up.
4. **The card reveal animation is your best moment.** Make sure it's visible and given space to breathe.
5. **Record 2-3 takes.** Pick the best one. It's worth 15 extra minutes.
