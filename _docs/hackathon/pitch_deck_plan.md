# Pitch Deck Plan (8 Slides)

**Context:** Used for Round 3 main stage presentation (3-5 min) and available as backup for table demo. Projected on a large screen to 200+ people.

**Tool:** Google Slides (for collaboration and backup) or Keynote. NOT PowerPoint on a USB — too many compatibility issues with venue laptops.

---

## Design Principles

- **One idea per slide.** If you need to explain a slide, it has too much on it.
- **Minimal text.** Maximum 6 words per line, maximum 3 lines per slide. The slide supports your voice — it doesn't replace it.
- **Large visuals.** Images, diagrams, screenshots should fill 60%+ of the slide.
- **Dark background recommended.** Works better on projectors in bright hackathon venues.
- **Consistent brand colors:**
  - Primary: `#4DA8FF` (Sky Blue)
  - Accent: `#FFD234` (Sunshine Yellow)
  - Action: `#FF6B6B` (Coral)
  - Background: `#0F172A` (Deep Navy) or `#1E293B` (Slate)
  - Text: `#F8FAFC` (White)

### SLIDE 2: The Guilt
**Time on stage:** 0:05 – 0:25 (20 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | A single photo/illustration: a child staring at a phone screen, glowing face in dim light. Or use `_concept.png` cropped to show the "before" moment. |
| **Text (large)** | "My daughter is 2½. She grabs my phone and demands YouTube." |

**Speaker notes:** This is the emotional hook. Deliver it slowly, make eye contact with the audience.

> "My daughter is two and a half years old. She already grabs my phone and demands to watch YouTube. Every time I hand it over, I feel a pang of guilt. She's absorbing passive content when she should be exploring the world. And I know — every parent in this room knows that feeling."

---

### SLIDE 3: The Problem
**Time on stage:** 0:25 – 0:45 (20 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | Stats appearing one by one (animated). Or a simple infographic. |
| **Stat 1** | 📱 Kids spend **3+ hours/day** on passive content |
| **Stat 2** | 🇻🇳 **8 million** Vietnamese kids learning English |
| **Stat 3** | 💰 Parents spend **$50-200/month** on tutoring |
| **Stat 4** | 📉 Vietnam ranks **58th** in English proficiency |

**Speaker notes:** Quick, punchy delivery. Each stat lands like a beat.

> "Kids spend hours daily on passive content — TikTok, YouTube, brainrot. In Vietnam, eight million children are learning English. Parents spend up to two hundred dollars a month on tutoring. Between classes? Nothing. Zero engaging practice. Vietnam ranks fifty-eighth globally in English proficiency. The spending is there. The results aren't."

---

### SLIDE 4: The Flip
**Time on stage:** 0:45 – 1:00 (15 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | Side-by-side: LEFT — muted, child slumped scrolling. RIGHT — bright, child pointing camera at the world. Use the concept art from `_docs/assets/_concept2.png` for the right side. |
| **Text (large, center)** | "What if that phone became an exploration tool?" |

**Speaker notes:** This is the turn. Voice shifts from problem to possibility.

> "But what if we flipped the script? What if that same phone — the thing parents feel guilty about — became the most powerful learning tool in the child's life?"

---

### SLIDE 5: The Demo
**Time on stage:** 1:00 – 2:15 (75 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | Pre-recorded screen capture plays full-screen (see `screen_recording_script.md`) |
| **Text** | None — video fills the slide |

**Speaker notes:** Video plays. You narrate live over it. Practice the timing so your words match the on-screen actions.

> "This is SnapLearn. I point the camera at a water bottle — snap — the AI identifies it in under three seconds. Vietnamese: 'Chai nước.' English: 'Water Bottle.' Common rarity, ten points."
>
> "The app speaks both words aloud — audio-first, because our users can't read yet. The child says it back. Three stars."
>
> "Now earbuds — Uncommon, twenty-five points. What happens when they mispronounce? No punishment. The mascot encourages them in Vietnamese. Second try — three stars."
>
> "And now — a toy excavator. Epic. One hundred points. *That's* what makes kids want to go to the market, the park, a museum. Not sit on the couch."
>
> "Every capture becomes a trophy in their collection. And they compete on a leaderboard."

---

### SLIDE 6: The Innovation
**Time on stage:** 2:15 – 2:40 (25 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | Simple diagram: CHILD ←→ 📷 CAMERA ←→ 🤖 AI, with "Shared Visual Context" label connecting all three. Or the mascot + child looking at the same object. |
| **Text (large)** | "Shared Visual Context" |
| **Subtext** | "The camera is a disguised prompt. The AI becomes a companion." |

**Speaker notes:** This is your differentiator. Speak with conviction.

> "Here's what we're really building. Most AI interactions are passive — you type a prompt, the AI responds. With SnapLearn, the camera creates shared visual context. The child doesn't feel like they're prompting an AI. They feel like they discovered something, and the AI is responding to that shared moment."
>
> "Imagine: the AI sees the same cat your child does, and asks — 'Is that your cat? What's its name?' Now it's a conversation. The physical world becomes the interface."

---

### SLIDE 7: The Vision
**Time on stage:** 2:40 – 2:55 (15 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | Roadmap with 3 steps. Include mascot image or concept art. |
| **Step 1** | 📱 **Now:** Vocabulary camera (English) |
| **Step 2** | 🏫 **Next:** Missions, parent dashboard, schools, Chinese |
| **Step 3** | 🧸 **Future:** Hardware devices, SnapBear plush toy, 8M kids |

**Speaker notes:** Fast. Don't dwell. This shows ambition without over-promising.

> "Today it's vocabulary. Next: Chinese language support, daily missions, school deployments. Long-term: dedicated hardware — a plush toy with a camera in its eye, completely screen-free — targeting Vietnam's eight million kids and a two-billion-dollar tutoring market."

---

### SLIDE 8: The Team + Close
**Time on stage:** 2:55 – 3:10 (15 seconds)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Visual** | Team photo or avatars. Mascot alongside. |
| **Text** | Team names + "4 AI Engineers. 1 Dad. 36 Hours." |
| **Closing tagline** | *"So parents can hand over the phone and feel proud — not guilty."* |

**Speaker notes:** End strong. The last sentence is the one they remember.

> "We're four enterprise AI engineers who build AI systems for a living. This hackathon, we built for the most demanding user we've ever faced — a six-year-old who can't read."
>
> *[Pause. Make eye contact.]*
>
> "SnapLearn. So parents can hand over the phone and feel proud — not guilty. Thank you."
